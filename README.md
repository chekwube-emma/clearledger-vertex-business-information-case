# Vertex Integrated Services
## Business Information & Management Visibility Case

> Project Type: Fictional demonstration consulting case

This project demonstrates how ClearLedger Business Solutions would approach a growing organisation with fragmented operational, commercial, workforce and financial information.

Vertex Integrated Services Ltd. is a fictional technical services company created specifically to demonstrate business information management, data quality, analytics, reporting and decision-support capabilities.

The objective was not simply to build a dashboard.

The objective was to understand why management was struggling to get a reliable view of the business, improve the underlying information structure, analyse performance and turn the data into useful management insight.

---

## The Business Problem

Vertex had grown rapidly, but its information processes had not matured at the same pace.

Operational and management information was spread across separate files maintained by different functions:

- Work orders
- Technician timesheets
- Job costs
- Client records
- Invoices
- Collections
- Management targets

Management had data, but answering important questions remained difficult.

Some of those questions included:

- Which clients are actually the most valuable?
- Which contracts generate strong margins?
- Are high-revenue clients also good cash-paying clients?
- Which service areas are missing agreed service levels?
- Where is overtime concentrated?
- Are teams being utilised efficiently?
- Which receivables require management attention?
- What should management prioritise?

---

## ClearLedger Approach

The project followed the ClearLedger information-management methodology:

Capture → Control → Analyse → Report → Improve

The work was completed in five stages:

1. Data quality and information-control diagnostic
2. Remediation and standardisation
3. Analysis-ready data preparation
4. KPI and management analysis
5. Dashboard and management recommendations

---

## 1. Data Quality Diagnosis

Before building any dashboard, the source data was reviewed for reliability.

The diagnostic identified issues including:

- Duplicate work orders
- Missing completion dates
- Inconsistent service classifications
- Inconsistent team names
- Invalid work-order references
- Impossible timesheet hours
- Negative job-cost transactions requiring review
- Client-name and Client-ID mismatches
- Missing invoice due dates
- Payment-status inconsistencies
- Poorly defined KPI target logic

The key finding was simple:

> A reporting problem can sometimes begin as an information-control problem.

Building management reporting directly on top of these issues could have produced misleading KPIs.

---

## 2. Data Remediation & Controls

Safe corrections were separated from records requiring business confirmation.

Examples of remediation included:

- Standardising service categories
- Standardising team names
- Standardising cost categories
- Using controlled master-data references
- Deriving client names from Client IDs
- Calculating invoice due dates from payment terms
- Deriving payment status from financial values
- Creating controlled labour-rate structures
- Improving KPI target rules
- Quarantining unresolved records instead of silently changing them

This preserved the distinction between data cleaning and data governance.

| Contribution Margin | ₦111.5m | — |
| Contribution Margin % | 23.5% | ≥25% |
| Collection Rate | 68.0% | ≥90% |
| Outstanding Receivables | ₦126.7m | — |
| Completed Work Orders | 804 | — |
| SLA Compliance | 88.8% | ≥90% |

The analysis showed that first-time resolution was above target, while contribution margin, collections and SLA compliance required management attention.

---

## Key Business Insight

### The largest customer was not necessarily the most valuable customer.

Meridian Foods generated approximately ₦52.4m in billable revenue.

However:

- Contribution Margin: approximately 21.0%
- Collection Rate: approximately 45.3%
- Outstanding Receivables: approximately ₦25.0m

NovaCare Hospitals generated significantly less revenue at approximately ₦28.2m, but showed:

- Contribution Margin: approximately 24.5%
- Collection Rate: approximately 87.6%
- Outstanding Receivables: approximately ₦3.3m

This demonstrated why client performance should not be evaluated using revenue alone.

Management also needs to consider:

Revenue + Cost-to-Serve + Margin + Service Performance + Cash Conversion

---

## Workforce Insight

The workforce analysis produced another important finding.

Team Delta had the highest overall utilisation, while Team Bravo recorded the highest overtime rate.

At the same time, all teams remained below the intended 75–85% utilisation range.

This means the data did not automatically support a conclusion that Vertex needed additional staff.

Instead, management should investigate:

- Workload distribution
- Scheduling efficiency
- Skill bottlenecks
- Productive-time capture
- Resource allocation

This illustrates an important analytics principle:

> Good analysis should challenge assumptions rather than confirm them automatically.

---

## Management Recommendations

The analysis suggested management should prioritise:

1. Strengthening receivables and collection management
2. Reviewing high-revenue but low-margin clients
3. Investigating workload distribution and overtime concentration
4. Improving SLA performance in weaker service categories
5. Maintaining controlled master-data structures
6. Automating recurring management-report consolidation where appropriate
